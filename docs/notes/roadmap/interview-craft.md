# Interview Craft

*Part of the [Interview Prep Roadmap](./). Coverage ratings are explained there.*

## Aptitude & Logical Puzzles
Number series, probability puzzles, and classic brainteasers — the kind of reasoning questions that often open a screening round.

*Coverage: moderate*

### Quantitative Aptitude
- Ratios and proportions
- Percentages, profit, and loss
- Time, speed, and distance
- Work and rate problems

### Number Series & Patterns
- Arithmetic and geometric progressions
- Series completion
- Analogies and odd-one-out

### Logical Reasoning
- Seating arrangements and ordering
- Blood relations and direction sense
- Syllogisms
- Coding-decoding

### Classic Puzzles
- Weighing and measuring puzzles
- Probability puzzles
- Minimum-tests and minimum-moves optimization
- Geometric figure counting

## Behavioral & HR Questions
Why this company, why you're leaving your current role, strengths and weaknesses — the non-technical half of the process.

*Coverage: moderate*

### Self Presentation
- Introducing yourself
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Career goals

### Motivation & Fit
- Interest in the company
- Reason for leaving a current role
- Salary expectations

### Experience & Projects
- Walking through a past project
- Technical decisions and their trade-offs
- Failure and debugging stories

### Situational & Teamwork
- Conflict with a teammate
- Disagreement with a technical decision
- Working under deadline pressure
- Handling difficult stakeholders

### Questions for the Interviewer
- Evaluating the team and the role
- Reverse-interview topics
