# Interview Prep Roadmap

A structured path through this question bank, topic by topic, instead of company by company. Each topic below will eventually link straight to the specific questions that cover it.

This page is being built in stages: topics first, then sub-topics within each, then direct links to practice questions. Every topic and sub-topic is meant to be a complete learning path on its own — basics before the advanced material that builds on them — not just a reflection of whichever questions happen to exist. Some topics have little or nothing in the bank yet — they're listed anyway, because they belong in a complete roadmap regardless of what we currently have to practice with. The **Coverage** line is where that honesty lives, kept separate from the topic description itself:

- **Rich** — well covered, multiple companies, enough to learn from
- **Moderate** — covered, but thinner or concentrated in fewer sources
- **Thin** — a handful of real questions, not enough to rely on alone
- **Gap** — essentially nothing yet; included as a placeholder for future contributions
